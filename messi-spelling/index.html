📢❓ Lesson 1 · Four kinds of sentences

أنواع الجمل الأربعة

A sentence is a group of words that tells a complete idea. It starts with a capital letter and ends with an end mark.

📢 Declarative خبرية
tells something → ends with .
The ship sailed across the ocean.
❓ Interrogative استفهامية
asks a question → ends with ?
What color is the dog?
👉 Imperative أمرية
gives a command or request → ends with . (the subject you is hidden)
Open the door, please.
😲 Exclamatory تعجبية
shows strong feeling → ends with !
What a hard test that was!

Question words: who, what, where, when, why, how, which, is, are, do, does, did, can, could, will, should, may…

Change the type: Maria will be home soon. → Will Maria be home soon?

✂️ Lesson 2 · Subjects and predicates

المبتدأ (الفاعل) والخبر (المُسنَد)

The subject tells who or what the sentence is about. The predicate tells what the subject does or is.

The brown dog | barked loudly.
  • Complete subject: all the subject words → The brown dog
  • Simple subject: the main word → dog
  • Complete predicate: all the predicate words → barked loudly
  • Simple predicate: the main verb → barked (it can be 2 words: can learn, is hanging)

🧩 Fragment جملة ناقصة: not a complete thought — Running through the park.
🚂 Run-on جملتان بدون فاصل: two sentences stuck together — I went to the park I played on the swings.
Fix a run-on: I went to the park. I played on the swings. or I went to the park, and I played on the swings.

🏷️ Lesson 3 · Common and proper nouns

الاسم العام واسم العَلَم

🏷️ Common noun اسم عام
a general name → no capital
teacher, city, book, happiness
👑 Proper noun اسم عَلَم
a special name → Capital letter!
Emma, New York, Monday, NASA
  • Capitalize days and months: Class meets on Mondays in May.
  • Capitalize the first and important words in a title: The Wind in the Willows
  • Abbreviations start with a capital and often end with a period: Dr., Mr., Ms., Ave., St., Jan.

👯 Even and odd numbers الأعداد الزوجية والفردية

Even numbers make pairs with nobody left over: ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽ 6 is even.
Odd numbers have one left over: ⚽⚽⚽⚽⚽ 5 is odd.

Look at the ones digit: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 → even · 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 → odd

By rows: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12   By columns: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12

Rows go across ➡️. Columns go up and down ⬇️. Both ways give the same total!

💯 Add on a hundred chart الجمع على جدول المئة

Find 54 + 18: start at 54. ⬇️ Move down 1 row to add 1 ten (64). ➡️ Move ahead 8 to add 8 ones (72). So 54 + 18 = 72.

🤝 Friendly numbers الأعداد الصديقة

29 + 14: 29 is close to 30. 29 + 1 = 30, so take 1 from 14: 14 − 1 = 13. Now 30 + 13 = 43. Easy!

🧱 Add with tens and ones الجمع بالعشرات والآحاد

47 + 26: join the tens: 4 + 2 = 6 tens. Join the ones: 7 + 6 = 13 ones. 13 ones is too many — regroup 10 ones as 1 ten → 7 tens 3 ones = 73.
